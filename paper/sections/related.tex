\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Self-training between deployments.}
Filtering a model's own samples by a correctness signal and fine-tuning on the
survivors is a proven recipe: STaR bootstraps rationales that yield correct
answers---its ``rationalization'' step, generating with the answer as a hint,
prefigures our hint-conditioned teacher \citep{zelikman2022star}; ReST and
ReST-EM formalize the sample--filter--train loop as growing-batch RL and EM with
binary verifiers \citep{gulcehre2023rest, singh2024beyond}; rejection-sampling
fine-tuning applies it at scale \citep{yuan2023scaling}; SPIN and Self-Rewarding
LMs replace the verifier with self-play or self-judgment \citep{chen2024spin,
yuan2024selfrewarding}. Recent variants push toward autonomy---SEAL learns to
emit its own fine-tuning data \citep{zweiger2025seal}, TTRL runs RL on unlabeled
test sets with majority-vote pseudo-rewards \citep{zuo2025ttrl}, and Absolute
Zero self-plays against an executor \citep{zhao2025absolute}. All of these
require the task corpus upfront and multiple sweeps over it (several restart
from the base model each round to control drift). \emph{Unlike them, \method{}
improves during a single pass: each problem is seen once, and the model
answering problem $t{+}1$ is permanently better than the one that answered
problem $t$.}

\paragraph{Streaming adaptation without weight updates.}
The dominant approach to deployment-time improvement stores what was learned
\emph{outside} the model: reflection buffers \citep{shinn2023reflexion}, skill
libraries \citep{wang2024voyager}, experiential insights \citep{zhao2024expel},
induced workflows \citep{wang2025agent}, feedback memories
\citep{madaan2022memprompt}, and evolving context playbooks---the last framed
explicitly as an \emph{alternative} to parameter updates \citep{zhang2025agentic}.
StreamBench \citep{wu2024streambench} canonized streaming evaluation and found
Self-StreamICL---a reward-filtered memory retrieved as demonstrations---the
strongest such baseline; it is our ICL baseline and the source of our memory's
correctness filter. These methods are capped by the frozen model, lose
everything without their memory, and can be misled by retrieved neighbors
(\S\ref{sec:exp-bench}). \emph{Unlike them, \method{} uses the same filtered
memory as a source of supervision, so gains persist in the weights---our
mastery evaluation measures exactly this residue after all scaffolding is
removed.}

\paragraph{Test-time and continual weight adaptation.}
Test-time training updates weights per instance and discards them
\citep{sun2020ttt}, including LoRA-based variants for few-shot reasoning
\citep{akyurek2025surprising} and active test-time fine-tuning on retrieved
corpus data \citep{hubotter2025sift}. Continual-learning methods retain updates
but assume task-segmented, labeled datasets \citep{shi2025continual,
parisi2019continual}, and naive continual fine-tuning forgets
\citep{luo2023empirical}. \emph{Unlike transient TTT, our updates are
cumulative; unlike CL, our supervision is self-generated from a binary bit with
no task boundaries; and the distribution-gap account of forgetting
\citep{yang2024selfdistillation} explains why our self-generated targets update
safely where gold-target fine-tuning would not.}

\paragraph{Distillation and RL from verifiable rewards.}
On-policy distillation trains the student on its own samples against a teacher's
distribution \citep{agarwal2024gkd, gu2024minillm, xu2025skd, lu2025onpolicy}
---but the teacher is an external, stronger, fixed model and training is
offline. SDFT rewrites gold datasets into the model's own distribution before
fine-tuning \citep{yang2024selfdistillation}---offline and gold-dependent. SDPO,
closest to our objective, makes the feedback-conditioned model a self-teacher
inside policy optimization \citep{hue2026sdpo}---but remains rollout-batch RL on
a fixed prompt distribution with a \emph{moving} teacher. On the RL side, GRPO,
RLOO, and REINFORCE++ optimize the scalar verifiable bit
\citep{shao2024deepseekmath, ahmadian2024rloo, hu2025reinforcepp,
ouyang2022instructgpt}, and entropy/policy collapse on long horizons is a
documented failure mode requiring explicit KL control or reference resets
\citep{cui2025entropy, liu2025prorl}. \emph{Unlike SDPO we distill against a
frozen-base teacher---an always-on reference anchor rather than periodic
resets---and unlike all of the above we do it online: to our knowledge no prior
work performs verified, weight-updating self-distillation over a deployment
stream (the nearest streaming weight-update systems are SFT-style without a
self-teacher). Our experiments make the contrast operational: the same verified
bit that collapses REINFORCE++ on a long mixed stream trains \method{} stably
(\S\ref{sec:exp-fused}), and the same loss run as batch epochs underperforms
the stream (\S\ref{sec:exp-transfer}).}
